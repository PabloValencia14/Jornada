# Jornada
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
android
## Stack
Native Kotlin, Jetpack Compose Material 3, platform SQLite. Technology chosen to deliver requested lightweight Android app; no server, account, analytics or Internet permission.
## Users
One person keeping a reliable personal record of work attendance, primarily on an Android phone. Tablet support and testing requested.
## Product Purpose
Clock in and out, define effective work schedules, inspect calendar and lateness, annotate exceptional cases and export any date range or all records to Excel.
## Operating Context
Offline personal Android device. Public GitHub source and installable APK requested. ADB testing authorized on Xiaomi Pad 7 only.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Local durable data, versioned schedules, split and overnight shifts, manual corrections with reasons and audit history, day exceptions, XLSX export. Lightweight; no background service. Device clock is the source of time; personal record, not a certified employer time system.
## Brand Commitments
User approved warm ivory, dark text, restrained green, clear agenda and prominent clock-in action. Spanish UI. Functional language only: no slogans or promotional copy.
## Product Principles
Fast everyday action; original timestamps remain auditable; no fictitious user data; explicit export; mobile first.
